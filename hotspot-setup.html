Hotspot Deployment Prerequisites

Everything required to launch a high-speed commercial Wi-Fi hotspot with automated M-Pesa billing and Mikrotik bandwidth management.

Core Router Hardware

  • Mikrotik RouterBoard: hEX rev3 (small setup), RB3011 / RB4011 (medium/large setup).
  • Gigabit Switch: 8-port to 24-port PoE switch for powered AP connectivity.
  • CAT6 Ethernet Cable: Pure copper outdoor SFTP cable for signal integrity.
  • RJ45 Connectors & Boots: Shielded crimps to prevent static damage.

Access Points (AP)

  • Outdoor High-Power APs: TP-Link EAP225 / EAP610, Ruijie Reyee RAP2260, Tenda i24.
  • Dual-Band Coverage: 2.4GHz for wide range + 5GHz for high speed.
  • PoE Injectors / Adapters: 24V or 48V Gigabit Passive/802.3af PoE.
  • Mounting Hardware: Galvanized steel poles, wall brackets & hose clamps.

Bandwidth & Power

  • Dedicated WAN Source: Fiber connection (FTTH/Dedicated) or high-capacity PTP link.
  • Minimum Speeds: 30Mbps to 100Mbps+ depending on client capacity.
  • Power Backup (UPS): 650VA+ UPS or 12V DC UPS to keep routers alive during outages.
  • Surge Protection: Grounded power extensions and lightning arrestors.

Billing & Software

  • M-Pesa Integration: Automated Paybill / Till Number STK push integration.
  • Radius / Billing Gateway: Vouchers, hourly/daily/monthly packages & auto-disconnect.
  • Branded Captive Portal: Custom login page with venue logo, rates & promo banners.
  • Speed Control Queues: Per-user bandwidth limiting (e.g., 3Mbps per user).
