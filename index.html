About Us

Furaha Technologies delivers reliable fiber and wireless connectivity across Kenya — combining local expertise with enterprise-grade processes to design, deploy and support networks that last.

Founded and operated in Nakuru, our team specialises in end-to-end telecom delivery: from field surveys and CAD designs to tower builds, fiber splicing and hotspot billing integrations. We partner with municipalities, ISPs and enterprises to bring affordable, high-performance connectivity to underserved areas.
